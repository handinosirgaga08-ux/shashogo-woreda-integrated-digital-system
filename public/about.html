About SWIDS

The official digital portal for Shashogo Woreda administrative records, built to make local government services faster, organized and transparent.

Our purpose

SWIDS brings the Woreda's records, reports and public notices into one secure online system, so residents and staff spend less time on paperwork and can see clearly how services are delivered.

What you can do here

  • Read official Woreda announcements and news.
  • Keep and manage administrative records by kebele and sector.
  • Submit annual plans and quarterly performance reports online.
  • Review reports and give feedback, and follow evaluations and results.
  • Send citizen reports to the Woreda administration.

Who uses SWIDS

  • Woreda administrators: manage kebeles, sectors and user accounts.
  • Sector heads: review sector reports and give feedback.
  • Sector officers: submit plans and performance reports.
  • Kebele officers: manage records for their kebele.
  • Residents: read announcements and send citizen reports.
