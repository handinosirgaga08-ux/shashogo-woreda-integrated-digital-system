Welcome to SWIDS

The official digital portal for Shashogo Woreda administrative records, built to make local government services faster, organized and transparent.

About the Software

SWIDS brings the Woreda's records and daily administrative work into one secure system.

Purpose: to replace scattered paper files with a single digital system where officials can register, store, search and manage administrative records accurately, and where residents get quicker, more reliable service.

Key Benefits

⚡

Faster service

Find and update records in seconds instead of searching through paper files.

🔒

Secure records

Role-based admin access keeps sensitive data protected from unauthorized changes.

🗂️

Organized data

All records are kept in one central place, reducing loss, duplication and errors.

🔍

Transparency

Clear, traceable records support accountability and public trust.

📱

Accessible anywhere

Works on phones and computers, with a mobile app for easy daily use.

📊

Better decisions

Accurate, up-to-date information helps leaders plan and report with confidence.
